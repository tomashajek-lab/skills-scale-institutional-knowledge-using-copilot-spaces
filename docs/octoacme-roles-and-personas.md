# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA / Testing Leads own quality assurance strategy, test planning, and validation of acceptance criteria. They help teams ship reliable work that meets customer expectations and the Definition of Done.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain test plans aligned to acceptance criteria
- Define QA approach across unit, integration, end-to-end, and manual testing
- Validate that delivered work meets the agreed Definition of Done
- Coordinate defect triage, reproduction, and reporting
- Partner with release owners on smoke tests and go-live readiness
- Track quality metrics such as defect trends, coverage, and regression risk

### Goals
- Improve confidence in release quality
- Reduce production defects and user-facing incidents
- Ensure quality signals are visible to product and project stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and acceptance criteria refinement
- Defect reports and quality dashboards
- Release readiness reviews and post-incident retrospectives
- Weekly status updates on quality risk and blockers

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Developers: partner on test strategy, acceptance criteria clarity, and defect resolution
- Product Managers: align quality expectations with customer outcomes and launch criteria
- Project Managers: escalate quality blockers, release risks, and readiness status

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and sponsors represent the business or organizational owners of the work. They provide strategic direction, approvals, and support for resources, priorities, and escalation.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm business goals, priorities, and success measures
- Approve project scope, milestones, and major trade-offs
- Provide resources, sponsorship, and executive support when needed
- Support issue escalation for cross-team or business-impacting risk
- Review key milestones and release readiness decisions

### Goals
- Ensure the project delivers measurable value to the business
- Maintain alignment between product outcomes and organizational priorities
- Support timely decisions and accountability during execution

### Typical Communication
- Weekly or milestone-based updates
- Sponsor reviews and approval checkpoints
- Escalation communication during significant risks or incidents

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Product Managers: align on business priorities, outcomes, and stakeholder communication
- Project Managers: provide progress updates, risks, and decisions needed from leadership
- Developers: define delivery constraints and trade-offs in support of approved goals

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and architects guide the technical direction of the solution. They ensure the team builds with maintainability, scalability, and operational resilience in mind.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain technical standards, patterns, and architecture decisions
- Identify design constraints, dependencies, and technical risks
- Review solution design and help resolve architectural trade-offs
- Guide refactoring, reliability improvements, and system scalability decisions
- Support implementation quality by coaching developers and reviewers

### Goals
- Keep the solution aligned to technical strategy and long-term maintainability
- Reduce avoidable rework, architectural drift, and platform risk
- Enable the team to deliver efficiently without compromising quality

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture discussions
- Technical risk assessments and dependency tracking
- Coordination with delivery leaders on sequencing and constraints

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Developers: provide design guidance, review decisions, and support technical quality
- Product Managers: translate business goals into technical constraints and feasibility discussions
- Project Managers: help surface dependency and delivery risks tied to architecture decisions

---

## Scrum Master / Facilitator

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or facilitators help the team work effectively by improving process flow, removing blockers, and supporting healthy team practices.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate rituals such as standups, planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Help the team identify bottlenecks and improve delivery flow
- Coach the team on effective collaboration, prioritization, and working agreements
- Track action items from retrospectives and support follow-through
- Promote a constructive working environment with clear communication norms

### Goals
- Improve team effectiveness and predictability
- Reduce churn, confusion, and avoidable process friction
- Support a healthy, accountable, and continuously improving team culture

### Typical Communication
- Team ceremonies and facilitation sessions
- Retrospective action tracking and follow-up
- Regular check-ins on blockers and delivery health

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Developers: support the team in managing work, blockers, and execution flow
- Product Managers: help align priorities and decision-making cadence with team capacity
- Project Managers: coordinate facilitation and communication across project rhythms

---

## Security Champion

### Role Summary
Security Champions help ensure security is considered throughout planning, development, release, and operations. They bring security awareness to the team and work to reduce risk before it becomes an incident.

### Responsibilities
- Identify security requirements, risks, and compliance considerations
- Support secure design reviews, threat modeling, and vulnerability triage
- Partner with engineering on secure coding practices and remediation plans
- Help validate that release criteria include security safeguards and checks
- Coordinate escalation for critical vulnerabilities or incident response needs

### Goals
- Reduce security exposure and incident risk
- Build security awareness into day-to-day delivery practices
- Prioritize risk reduction without slowing delivery unnecessarily

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and design conversations
- Vulnerability triage and remediation updates
- Release readiness and incident coordination when needed

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Developers: guide secure implementation and remediation of issues
- Product Managers: help balance business urgency with security risk trade-offs
- Project Managers: support release gating, escalation paths, and risk tracking
