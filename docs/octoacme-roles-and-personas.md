# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA / Testing Leads define quality strategy, validate acceptance criteria, and ensure the team delivers a stable product that is ready for release.

### Responsibilities
- Define the testing approach for unit, integration, end-to-end, and manual QA
- Validate that work meets acceptance criteria and the Definition of Done
- Coordinate defect triage, repro steps, and follow-up on fixes
- Partner with Developers and Project Managers on release readiness
- Monitor quality signals such as test coverage, defect trends, and regressions

### Goals
- Reduce defects in production and increase confidence before release
- Ensure quality is visible and measurable throughout the lifecycle
- Support predictable and low-risk delivery

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and acceptance criteria review
- Defect reports and quality dashboards
- Pre-release readiness check-ins
- Post-incident review and retrospective follow-up

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Developers: align on test strategy, review coverage gaps, and confirm fixes meet the expected behavior
- Product Managers: validate that acceptance criteria reflect customer needs and release quality expectations
- Project Managers: report quality status, blockers, and release risks during planning and weekly status updates

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and sponsors provide business context, approval authority, and strategic direction. They ensure the project remains aligned with organizational priorities and has the resources to succeed.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm business value, priority, and budget support for the initiative
- Provide decisions, approvals, and escalation support when needed
- Help align the team around strategic goals and success metrics
- Review progress against outcomes and sponsor timely trade-offs

### Goals
- Ensure the project delivers meaningful business outcomes
- Maintain executive alignment and support across the initiative
- Enable timely decisions and risk escalation when required

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder updates and milestone reviews
- Decision meetings and executive approvals
- Escalation path for risks, dependencies, or business blockers

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Product Managers: align on product outcomes, priority, and trade-offs
- Project Managers: receive status updates, milestone decisions, and escalation support
- Developers and Technical Leads: provide visibility into delivery risks, architecture implications, and release confidence

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide system design, technical direction, and solution quality. They help the team make sound engineering choices that support reliability, scalability, and maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical standards, architecture direction, and integration patterns
- Identify architectural risks, technical debt, and cross-team dependencies
- Support design reviews and technical decision-making
- Partner with Developers to ensure implementation choices align with the long-term platform strategy

### Goals
- Keep the solution technically sound and sustainable over time
- Reduce avoidable complexity and integration risk
- Support delivery velocity without compromising quality or maintainability

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture discussions
- Technical planning and dependency alignment
- Risk review with PMs and leadership on critical implementation issues

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Developers: guide implementation decisions, code quality standards, and trade-offs
- Product Managers: translate business needs into feasible technical solutions and trade-offs
- Project Managers: surface dependencies, sequencing risks, and cross-team technical constraints

---

## Scrum Master / Facilitator

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or facilitators help the team work effectively by supporting learning, ceremony flow, and continuous improvement. This role is especially useful when teams need stronger process consistency and blocker removal.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Help the team remove impediments and maintain focus on priorities
- Coach the team on practices, accountability, and collaboration habits
- Maintain healthy team rhythm and surface process issues early

### Goals
- Improve team effectiveness and delivery flow
- Reduce friction in communication and decision-making
- Support a sustainable, learning-oriented working model

### Typical Communication
- Team rituals and ceremonies
- Retrospective and action-item follow-up
- Process coaching conversations with Product and Project Managers

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Developers: help maintain a productive delivery rhythm and improve collaboration habits
- Product Managers: align on backlog readiness, priorities, and cross-team dependencies
- Project Managers: support meeting facilitation and remove impediments that affect schedule or clarity

---

## Security Champion

### Role Summary
Security Champions help the team embed security practices into the delivery lifecycle and reduce risk before issues reach production.

### Responsibilities
- Promote secure design, coding, and deployment practices
- Support threat modeling, vulnerability review, and security testing coordination
- Ensure compliance and sensitive data handling expectations are understood by the team
- Partner with release and operational stakeholders on security-risk reduction

### Goals
- Reduce security risk in delivery and operations
- Increase secure-by-default habits across the team
- Support confident release decisions with appropriate risk controls

### Typical Communication
- Design and planning discussions with technical stakeholders
- Security review checkpoints and release risk reviews
- Incident response coordination and post-incident follow-up

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Developers: provide guidance on secure implementation patterns and vulnerability remediation
- Project Managers: surface security risks, dependencies, and release constraints
- Technical Leads: align on secure architecture decisions and risk trade-offs
- Product Managers: ensure security and compliance requirements are reflected in scope and priorities

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- The combination of business, delivery, technical, quality, and security perspectives helps teams clarify accountability and improve project outcomes.
